Settings

Stored in data/settings.json on this computer. Environment variables (SPLUNK_BASE, SPLUNK_LOCALE, SPLUNK_APP, VOYAGER_URL) override them.

Splunk

The address you open in the browser (Splunk Web), without the locale part.
Locale: the part after the host in Splunk URLs, e.g. en-GB in /en-GB/app/search. App: where searches run, so your macros and lookups resolve.
Both analyses can run at once, so Splunk sees up to twice the per-module number. Stay under your search quota.

Browser session bridge

Voyager uses your browser's Splunk session (single sign-on included); it never sees your password. A userscript in Tampermonkey sends the session cookies to this app while a Splunk tab is open.

  1. Install Tampermonkey (the BETA build is needed for HTTP-only cookies).
  2. Save the Splunk URL here, then install the userscript. It only runs on your Splunk URL.
  3. Open any Splunk page. The session appears in the header of this app within 30 seconds.
  4. For long runs, open one Splunk tab with #voyager at the end of its URL: that tab becomes the keep-alive tab and reloads itself when the session expires.
Voyager listens on this computer only. It refuses requests from other web pages, except the session cookies from your Splunk URL.
