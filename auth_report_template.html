Summary

Logon attempts per hour (UTC)

Accounts

One row per account and target. "Success after failure": the first successful logon at or after the first failure of that account on that target. Click a row for its events and the raw evidence.

Sources

Source address (or client host when the address is not logged). "Peak in 10 min": most failures from that source within any 10 minutes. "Most failures on one account": the highest failure count of a single account from that source.

Listener connections

From the listener logs: connection requests, not authentications. The client user and program are those the client reported.

Outbound connection failures

From the alert logs: connections the logging host failed to open to other databases ("Fatal NI connect error").

Alert-log logons

"logon successful" blocks of the alert logs, listed as written (not counted with the audit logons above).
